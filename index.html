100% Free Hosting & Phone App Setup

Deploy this exact app online in under 5 minutes without spending a dime.

Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended)

  1. Create a free account on GitHub.com.
  2. Create a new repository named my-planner.
  3. Upload this index.html file directly.
  4. Go to Settings > Pages, select main branch, and click Save.
  5. Your app is now live worldwide at https://yourusername.github.io/my-planner!

Add Shortcut to Android & Mobile Screen

  1. Open your hosted website link on your Android phone's Google Chrome app.
  2. Tap the 3 dots menu at the top right of Chrome.
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
  4. Your planner will now run like a native mobile app straight from your home screen!

Alexa Voice Reminders Integration

Enable the free **Any.do** or **Todoist** Alexa skill on your Amazon Alexa App. Any voice command to Alexa automatically updates your linked task lists across all platforms!
